Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026

Pritze provides salon discovery, booking, and partner management for customers, salon owners, and barbers. This policy explains the information used to provide those services.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as name, phone number, email address, role, and sign-in provider.
  • Authentication information used for phone OTP, email OTP, or Google sign-in.
  • Booking details including salon, service, barber, appointment time, price, status, and customer contact details.
  • Salon and staff information including business contact details, operating hours, services, prices, availability, and assignments.
  • Approximate or precise location when permission is granted, used to show relevant nearby salons and distance.
  • Device identifiers and notification tokens used to deliver account and booking notifications.
  • Technical, security, and diagnostic data processed by Firebase and Google services.

How we use information

We use information to authenticate users, show salons and appointment availability, create and manage bookings, help salon partners manage their business, deliver notifications, provide support, protect accounts, and maintain the app.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Booking details are shared with the relevant salon and assigned barber as needed to provide the service. Salon and staff details are shown to customers where needed for discovery and booking.

Service providers

Pritze uses Google and Firebase services for authentication, cloud data storage, server functions, notifications, security, and related app infrastructure. Their processing is governed by the Firebase privacy information and Google Privacy Policy.

Storage, security, and retention

App data is stored using Google Firebase services. We use authentication and access rules to restrict data based on user identity and role. We retain information as needed to provide the app, maintain business records, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

Your choices

You can decline optional location and notification permissions in your device settings. You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information by contacting us. Some information may be retained where required for legal, security, fraud prevention, or legitimate business purposes.

Children

Pritze is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

International processing

Information may be processed on systems operated by Google Firebase and related providers outside your country or region.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date above will be changed when an update is published.
